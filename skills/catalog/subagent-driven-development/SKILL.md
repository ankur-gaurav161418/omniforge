# OmniForge Subagent Driven Development Skill

## Purpose
Decompose large tasks into isolated subtasks, dispatching subagents with explicit requirements and context.

## Guidelines
- Scope subagent prompts tightly to avoid context bloat.
- Provide clear verification criteria for each subagent.
- Collect and synthesize subagent outputs before committing changes.
